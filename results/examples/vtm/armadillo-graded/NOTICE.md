# Dataset attribution and terms

These data and images derive from the Stanford Armadillo model, distributed by
libigl tutorial-data. Credit: Stanford University Computer Graphics Laboratory.
The original source commit, SHA256 and our coordinate normalization are recorded
in this example's input-provenance.json. Connectivity was not repaired or
simplified by Warpower. Armadillo is an additional scanned benchmark, not a model
shown in the Alliez VTM paper.

The Stanford terms permit research use and free redistribution with source
acknowledgment. Commercial use requires Stanford permission. These data and
images are not relicensed under the Warpower code license. See the [Stanford terms](https://graphics.stanford.edu/data/3Dscanrep/) and
[pinned libigl attribution](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/libigl/libigl-tutorial-data/644dd4104843b6d736745d9dafbd70bf8d175648/README.md).
