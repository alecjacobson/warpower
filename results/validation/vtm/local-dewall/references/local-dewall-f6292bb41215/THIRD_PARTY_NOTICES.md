# Third-party notices

## Robust geometric predicates

The expansion-arithmetic macros and robust-predicate routines in
`include/predicates.h` are based on the public-domain robust predicates by
Jonathan Richard Shewchuk:

> Jonathan Richard Shewchuk. Adaptive Precision Floating-Point Arithmetic and
> Fast Robust Geometric Predicates. *Discrete & Computational Geometry*,
> 18(3):305-363, 1997.

Project page and original software:
<https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~quake/robust.html>

The original predicate code is stated by its author to be in the public domain.
